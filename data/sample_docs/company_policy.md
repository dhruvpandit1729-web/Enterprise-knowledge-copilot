# Sample Enterprise Policy Document

## Remote Work Guidelines
Employees are permitted to work remotely up to two days per week with prior manager approval. All remote work arrangements must maintain core working hours between 10:00 AM and 4:00 PM local time.

## Data Security and Confidentiality
All organizational documents, internal knowledge bases, and customer data must remain strictly confidential. Access credentials and API keys must never be committed to source control or shared over unsecured communication channels.

## Expense Reimbursement Policy
Standard business travel and hardware expenses incurred for official company business are fully reimbursable up to $500 per month, provided original receipts are submitted within 30 days of purchase.
